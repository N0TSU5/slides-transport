02 — The solution

Three AI engines that get freight moving

Graph search

Route optimisation

Dijkstra · initialising
startexploringsettledoptimal path

Roads become a weighted graph. Dijkstra (and A*) settle the cheapest node first until the destination is reached. Live traffic re-weights the edges, so routes re-plan in seconds.

Time-series neural nets

Demand prediction

actual demandforecast ± band

LSTM and transformer forecasters learn from order history, weather, holidays and events to predict demand by zone and hour, so vans, drivers and stock are in place before orders arrive.

Generative AI

Delivery efficiency

illustrative scenario

LLM agents turn data into action. They draft dispatch plans, re-sequence drops when disruption hits, explain decisions in plain language and write personalised customer updates.
